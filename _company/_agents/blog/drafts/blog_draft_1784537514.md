# 📝 블로그 포스팅 초안 ()

To use a formula in Excel, you must always start with an **equals sign (=)**. This tells Excel that you are entering a formula rather than plain text.

Here is a guide to getting started with formulas:

### 1. Basic Mathematical Operators
You can use Excel like a calculator by typing the following:
*   **Addition:** `=5+5`
*   **Subtraction:** `=10-3`
*   **Multiplication:** `=5*2` (uses the asterisk)
*   **Division:** `=20/4` (uses the forward slash)
*   **Exponents:** `=5^2` (5 squared)

---

### 2. Using Cell References (The "Power" of Excel)
Instead of typing numbers directly, you refer to the cells where the numbers are stored. If you change the number in the cell later, the formula updates automatically.

*   **Example:** Type `10` in cell **A1** and `20` in cell **B1**.
*   In cell **C1**, type: `=A1+B1`
*   Excel will display `30`. If you change the 10 to a 50, the result becomes 70 instantly.

---

### 3. Using Functions (Built-in Tools)
Excel has pre-built formulas called "Functions" that perform complex tasks. These always follow this syntax: `=FUNCTIONNAME(range_or_arguments)`.

*   **SUM:** Adds a range of cells.
    *   `=SUM(A1:A10)` (Adds everything from A1 to A10)
*   **AVERAGE:** Calculates the mean.
    *   `=AVERAGE(B1:B20)`
*   **COUNT:** Counts how many cells contain numbers.
    *   `=COUNT(A1:A50)`
*   **IF:** A logical test (If something is true, do this; otherwise, do that).
    *   `=IF(A1>50, "Pass", "Fail")`

---

### 4. Step-by-Step Instructions to Enter a Formula
1.  **Click the cell** where you want the result to appear.
2.  Type the **equals sign (=)**.
3.  Type the formula (e.g., `=SUM(A1:A5)`) or click the cells you want to include in the formula.
4.  Press **Enter** on your keyboard.

---

### Pro Tips:
*   **Use the Mouse:** Instead of typing "A1," you can simply click cell A1 while typing your formula. Excel will put it in for you.
*   **Check your work:** If you click on a cell with a formula, the formula will appear in the **Formula Bar** (the white bar at the top of the spreadsheet).
*   **Error Codes:** If you see `#NAME?`, you likely misspelled the function name. If you see `#VALUE!`, you are likely trying to add text and numbers together.
